# Parkhotelchecker v1.1 — market comparison view

Date: 2026-10-23
Status: slice 1 implemented (polling, add-markets action, ticket cadence);
comparison view pending.
Depends on: v1 plus the archive/delete and Telegram work

## Goal

Show the same hotel and arrival date as priced by every polled market, side by
side, so a market-specific offer or an unexpected divergence is visible without
creating a new track. Prices stay in their native currency; nothing is
converted.

This increment deliberately contains **no divergence alert**. The alert is a
later increment, built only after there is a multi-week baseline showing what
normal cross-market pricing looks like.

## Decisions locked

- Comparison markets poll every **72 h**. `es-es` stays at **6 h**.
- Comparison set: the 10 non-primary **EUR** markets (`fr-fr`, `it-it`,
  `de-de`, `nl-nl`, `pt-pt`, `en-ie`, `fr-be`, `nl-be`, `de-at`, `de-ch`) plus
  the two existing canaries (`en-gb`, `fr-ch`).
- `en-usd` and `da-dk` return prices (verified with `scripts/probe_markets.py`)
  but stay out of the comparison set: they are USD and DKK, and there is no FX.
- Native currency only. No FX source, no conversion, no cross-currency delta.
- The view ships first; the alert is a separate change after the baseline.
- The comparison view is **difference-first**: ES is the reference, only markets
  that differ appear by default, with a "show all markets" toggle. The probe
  found every EUR market identical for DCHH/2026-12, so a 13-column matrix is
  mostly noise.
- Existing active tracks can gain the comparison markets through an explicit,
  idempotent action. New tracks offer the same set in the create form.
- No database migration. `series`, `track_market`, `track_series` and
  `observation` already carry everything needed.

## Why 72 h and why all EUR markets

If the EUR markets really are identical, the extra polling is redundant by
design: it is a tripwire for the day one market stops matching, or for a
market-specific offer. An offer worth acting on for a trip months away does
not live for less than three days.

Cost at the maximum 31-arrival window, all 16 hotels, one track:

- one market is about 16 × 31 = 496 observation rows per poll;
- ES at 6 h is about 2,000 rows/day;
- 12 comparison markets at 72 h add about 2,000 rows/day in total.

At the default 7-day window it is a fraction of that. Request volume is about
12 markets × ~32 month-buckets / 3 days ≈ 130 requests/day, spread by the
existing per-bucket jitter.

## Current state (facts this plan builds on)

- `app/config.py`: `MARKET_CURRENCY`, `PRIMARY_MARKET = "es-es"`,
  `CANARY_MARKETS = ("en-gb", "fr-ch")`.
- Series identity already includes the market:
  `UNIQUE (hotel_code, market, arrival_date, los, party_id)` in `app/db.py`.
  The same hotel/date in another market is a distinct series with its own
  history, and adding markets does not touch existing observations.
- A fetch bucket is `(hotel, market, month, los, party)`; coverage is per
  market and cadence is `track_market.cadence_hours`.
- Tickets now use `max(24, track_market.cadence_hours)`, so a 72 h market's
  tickets no longer poll daily. ES keeps daily tickets.
- `tracks.add_markets` can add missing markets to an active, non-archived track
  and materialises only their series. Existing cadences are untouched.
- The dashboard still uses `queries.primary_market()`; the digest filters
  `s.market == config.PRIMARY_MARKET`.
- `app/web/templates/hotel.html` still hardcodes "prices in EUR" in the
  subtitle; the comparison slice must make it depend on the selected market.
- `scripts/probe_markets.py` checks every market in one hotel-month.
- 177 offline tests pass; `ruff --select F,E9` is clean.

## Acceptance criteria

- On the existing track, one action adds all missing comparison markets; the
  planner then polls them at 72 h, and ticket polling for them is no more
  frequent than 72 h.
- The hotel page shows the same arrival priced by every polled market, ES as
  the reference, deltas only among EUR markets, GBP/CHF shown without deltas.
- The market selector changes the chart and arrivals table without changing the
  comparison matrix.
- Telegram output is byte-for-byte unchanged in behaviour: ES only, shortlist
  only.
- No schema version bump, no new environment variable, no new external
  dependency.
- Existing history is untouched; adding markets creates new series only.

## Risks and mitigations

- **Some markets may not price some dates.** Blanks are data, not errors; the
  UI says "not seen". Do not infer a cheaper market from a missing cell.
- **Wide table on a phone.** Horizontal scroll, ES first, canaries last. If it
  is still unusable, split chart-only markets into a second small table.
- **Query cost.** If `comparison()` pushes the hotel page past ~100 ms on the
  NAS, add an index or restrict it to the visible window; measure before
  changing the schema.
- **Request volume.** 72 h across 12 markets is modest, but confirm the first
  cycles in Health/logs and watch for the 15-minute jitter spreading them.
- **Market-specific price differences are not automatically bad.** A difference
  may be a promotion, a tax, or a data anomaly. The view reports; it does not
  judge.
